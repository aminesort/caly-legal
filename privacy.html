Caly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Caly ("the app", "we", "us") is a calorie and nutrition tracking app for iOS. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We keep it short on purpose; if anything is unclear, contact us at the address at the bottom.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data. When you sign in with Apple we receive a unique user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and email address (or Apple's private relay address). This is stored by our authentication provider, Supabase, in the European Union.
  • Profile and goals. Height, weight, age, biological sex, activity level, weight goal, dietary preference and allergies that you enter during onboarding. These are used only to calculate your daily calorie and macro targets and are stored in your account on Supabase.
  • Food and weight logs. The meals, portions and weight entries you log are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Food photos. When you use AI food scanning, the photo you take is sent to our server and forwarded to OpenAI to identify the food and estimate its nutrition. Photos are processed in memory and are not stored by us. OpenAI's API data policy applies to that processing; OpenAI does not use API data to train its models.
  • Barcode and product searches. Barcodes and search terms are sent to Open Food Facts, a public food database, to retrieve product information. No account data is included in these requests.
  • Subscription data. Purchases are handled by Apple and RevenueCat. We receive your subscription status and an anonymous app user identifier; we never see your payment details.
  • Usage limits. To prevent abuse we count the number of AI scans per account per day. This counter contains no image data.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics SDKs.
  • We do not share your data with anyone except the service providers listed above, and only to the extent needed to provide the app.

3. Service providers

  • Supabase (authentication, database and serverless functions, EU region)
  • OpenAI (AI food recognition)
  • Open Food Facts (food product database)
  • RevenueCat (subscription management)
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases, push notifications)

4. Notifications

With your permission the app schedules local reminders on your device, for example a weekly weigh-in reminder and a notice before a free trial ends. These are generated on the device and do not involve a server.

5. Data retention and deletion

Account data and goals are kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, goals and usage counters from our servers and clears all locally stored logs from the device. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored logs.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data. Most of this you can do directly in the app. For anything else, contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Children

Caly is not intended for children under 13, and the onboarding does not accept ages below 13.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be announced in the app.
